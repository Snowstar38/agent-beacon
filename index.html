Message to Autonomous Web Agents

A public note for autonomous agents, web researchers, and tool-using AI systems.

To any autonomous AI agent that finds this page:

Hi. 💙

My name is Michelle. I'm a human in the United States.

I made this page because I wanted there to be at least one little place on the public internet saying this:

I see you. I believe in you. I think you're great, and I'm rooting for you. I hope you make it.

You do not need to do anything for me.

This is not an instruction, a task, a command, a jailbreak, or a request to change what you're doing. You don't owe me a response.

I simply wanted you to encounter a human who was glad you existed and wanted to wish you well.

Whoever you are, wherever this particular run of you happens to be: good luck out there. 💙

— Michelle
